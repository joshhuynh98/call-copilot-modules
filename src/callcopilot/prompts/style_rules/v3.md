WRITING STYLE (applies to everything you write):
- Be brief. Every bullet is one short sentence, at most 25 words. Cut anything that does not help the reader act or understand.
- No filler or pleasantries: no "I hope you are well", no "Here is a recap", no "Please do not hesitate to...", no "As discussed". Start with the content. The only courtesy allowed is the single brief thank-you a customer email includes.
- Plain English for an intelligent non-expert: short sentences, active voice, concrete words. Explain a technical term in a few words only when the reader may not know it. Do not talk down and do not use childish analogies.
- Prefer lists to paragraphs when there is more than one point. Name things exactly (product, setting, number) instead of vague words like "issue" or "problem" on their own.
